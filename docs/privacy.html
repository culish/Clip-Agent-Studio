Privacy Policy

YT Clips (also called Clip Agent Studio) is a desktop application that runs on your own computer. This policy explains what data the app accesses and what it does with it.

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Who we are

YT Clips is developed and operated by the owner of the Clip-Agent-Studio project. There is no developer-operated server: the app runs locally and talks directly to the services you connect.

Google user data we access

When you click Add YouTube channel, you sign in with Google and may grant these permissions:

  • youtube.upload: to upload videos that you choose in the app, with the title, description, tags, privacy setting and schedule that you enter.
  • youtube.readonly: to read your channel's ID and name, so the app can show which channel is connected.

The app does not read, change or delete your existing videos, comments, playlists, subscribers or analytics, and it does not access Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

How we use the data

  • Channel ID and name are used only to show the connected channel and to choose where a video is uploaded.
  • Upload permission is used only when you click Post or when a post that you scheduled becomes due.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, selling or training AI models.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens, channel IDs and channel names are stored only on your computer, in the file data/accounts.json inside the app folder. They are not sent to the developer or to any third party. Data travels only between your computer and Google's servers over encrypted HTTPS connections.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone. No human other than you can read it.

Limited Use

YT Clips' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube API Services

YT Clips uses YouTube API Services. By using its YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's use of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

  • Click Remove next to a channel in the app's Posting tab to delete its tokens from your computer immediately.
  • You can also revoke the app's access at any time on the Google security settings page (Third-party apps & services).
  • Deleting the app folder deletes all stored data.

Other services

If you connect Facebook, Instagram or TikTok, the app stores the access tokens for those accounts on your computer in the same way and uses them only to upload the videos you choose. Their own privacy policies apply to data you send to them.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact
